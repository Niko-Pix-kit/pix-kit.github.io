Pix-Moments Privacy Policy

Pix-Moments keeps track of days since, countdowns, deadlines, anniversaries and intermediate milestones, with personal widgets and optional reminders.

Summary

Pix-Moments stores your data on your Android device. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, remote crash reporting, or Pix-Kit cloud service. The app does not request Android’s Internet permission and does not send your Moments or photos to Pix-Kit.

1. Information Pix-Moments handles

Depending on the features you choose, the app stores:

  • Moment titles, dates, types, descriptions, emojis, categories, tags, colours, appearance settings, order, pinned status, and archive status.
  • Photos you select through Android’s photo picker, copied into the app’s private storage.
  • Milestone descriptions, fixed dates or day offsets, notification preferences and times.
  • Reminder dates, offsets and times, snooze dates, completion records, and reset history when enabled.
  • Theme, device-colour and language preferences, and your twelve most recently saved colours.
  • Local random identifiers, edit revisions, widget-to-Moment associations, and short-lived notification delivery records used to avoid duplicate reminders.

These identifiers are used locally for app behaviour. They are not advertising identifiers and are not transmitted to Pix-Kit.

2. Local storage and photos

Moments and settings are kept in the app’s private Android database. Selected photos are stored as resized copies in app-private storage. Pix-Moments does not modify or delete the original photos in your gallery.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app data. Moving to a different device requires an export and import that you request. The app does not apply a separate encryption password to its database or exported ZIP backups.

3. Notifications and widgets

Notifications are optional. On Android versions that require notification permission, the app asks before sending reminders. You can deny or revoke permission in Android settings. Milestone notifications include the description you entered for that step.

Widgets display the selected Moment’s emoji, title, counter and appearance on your home screen. Notifications may appear on your lock screen according to Android settings. Consider what other people can see on your device when adding personal information.

Android background scheduling is used to refresh widgets and deliver reminders, including after a restart or a time change. Battery restrictions, sleep and force-stop can delay this work. No exact-time alarm guarantee is made.

4. Export, import and selected providers

Exporting creates a ZIP containing your Moments, descriptions, milestones, history, preferences and referenced photo copies. The backup is not password-protected. You choose its destination using Android’s document picker.

A destination may be local storage or a cloud document provider installed on your device. That provider may upload and process your exported file under its own terms and privacy policy. Pix-Moments does not receive your provider password and does not connect directly to the provider over the Internet.

Import validates the selected backup and adds its Moments. Existing Moments are preserved; matching identifiers become separate copies. Supported appearance and language preferences from the backup are restored.

5. Data sharing and external links

Pix-Kit does not sell, rent, or use Pix-Moments data for advertising. No Moment data is sent to Pix-Kit. Data can leave app-private storage when you export a backup or when Android displays content through a widget or notification.

Opening this privacy policy launches your browser. Your browser, network and the website’s hosting provider handle that connection under their own practices. Visiting the policy does not upload your Moments or photos.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a Moment removes its database record, milestones and history. Its notifications are cancelled and its content is cleared from associated widgets. Android’s launcher controls removal of the widget’s home-screen slot.
  • Archiving keeps the Moment in the app and pauses its notifications. It does not delete the Moment.
  • Photo copies no longer referenced by any Moment are eligible for removal during background maintenance after seven days. Android may delay that maintenance. Original gallery photos are unaffected.
  • Notification delivery records are pruned during maintenance after seven days.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app’s private database and photo copies.
  • Exported backups remain at the destination you chose. Delete those copies yourself or through the selected provider.

7. Security

App-private storage, bounded input validation and transactional database writes help protect local data and reduce accidental corruption. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and keep exported backups in a destination you trust.

8. Children’s privacy

Pix-Moments is a general date-tracking utility. Pix-Kit does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this published version. Applicable store privacy disclosures will need to reflect any changes to data practices.

10. Contact

For questions about Pix-Moments or this privacy policy, contact niko@pix-kit.com.
